# ADR 0007 — Tuning-Curve Regularization Geometry (Review Set 1)

## Status

**Accepted (2026-07-09).** Covers Set 1 of the three-set post-implementation
review of the Prompt-F tuning-curve engines (`docs/design/tuning-curve-design.md`
is the governing spec; this ADR refines its §5/§6 mechanics without changing
any decision recorded there). Sets 2 (Giordano-layer fidelity) and 3
(engine-(d) derived weights) are queued in `docs/design/next-chats-handoff.md`.

## Context

The four curve engines shipped on 2026-07-09 (engines a–d in
`algorithms/tuning.rs`, with `inharmonicity.rs` / `dissonance.rs` /
`smoothing.rs` as their primitive layers). A same-day scrutiny pass against
the design note found three defects in the *regularization geometry* — the
part of the construction that is ours rather than ported:

1. **Unmeasured-tail extrapolation.** The Whittaker layer (engines b/c) and
   the multi-interval LS (engine d) penalize only the second difference of
   the residual from the prior. On the real captures the trusted-data region
   ends at key 56 (last key with ≥ 8 partials); above it the computed curve
   did **not** ride the prior as §8 of the design note requires — it carried
   the residual's end slope linearly to C8. Evidence: the (b)−(a) gap at
   A5/A6/A7 was −3.8/−8.1/−12.4 ¢ — an exact arithmetic progression
   (−4.3 ¢/octave), a 13 ¢ unintended swing at C8.
2. **Chain-gauge pinning.** Eq. 6 fixes only octave *differences*; each of
   the 12 semitone chains has an offset the data cannot identify. The
   implementation pinned each chain to the prior at one key in 37–48 and
   then fed that key to the smoother as a residual-0, weight-1 observation —
   twelve fabricated data points asserting "the data equals the prior here."
3. **`CurveInput::default()`** derived to an empty `Vec`, breaking the
   length-88 invariant every engine indexes against.

## Decision 1 — Prior-mean reversion term (fixes defect 1)

### Analysis (first principles)

Minimizing `∑ w_i·(y_i − z_i)² + λ·∑ (Δ²z_i)²` in a region where all
`w_i = 0`: the tail can zero every penalty row that touches it by choosing
`z_{K+1} = 2·z_K − z_{K−1}`, `z_{K+2} = 2·z_{K+1} − z_K`, … — the exact
linear continuation, at zero penalty cost and with no back-constraint on the
data region. So linear tail extrapolation is not solver error; it is the
objective *asserting infinite prior variance for the affine component of the
residual* (the null space of the second-difference operator). The design
note asserts the opposite: away from data, the residual's prior mean is zero
(§8 — "the treble rides the prior").

Corroboration: the Whittaker-smoother literature states extrapolated data
follow a polynomial of order d (= degree d−1, **linear** for our d = 2
penalty) — Eilers 2003. Verification status per the cite-and-verify rule:
the primary PDF is paywalled; the statement is corroborated by secondary
sources quoting the method (e.g. the `whittaker-eilers` implementation
documentation), by the derivation above (which stands alone), and by the
observed arithmetic progression on the real captures.

### The fix

Observe the prior mean (residual target 0) at every key carrying no data,
with weight w₀ — pseudo-observations declared **in the objective** (a proper
Gaussian prior; explicitly *not* an output clamp, which internals/04 bans).
The tail Euler–Lagrange equation of the augmented objective,

    w₀·z + λ·z'''' = 0,

has characteristic roots k = (w₀/λ)^(1/4)·(±1±i)/√2, so decaying solutions
fall off at rate (w₀/λ)^(1/4)/√2 and the reversion length is

    ℓ = √2·(λ/w₀)^(1/4)   ⇔   w₀ = 4λ/ℓ⁴.

Tying w₀ to λ keeps ℓ invariant across the CV grid: λ selection still
controls smoothness, ℓ controls only how fast the curve returns to the
prior where data ends. A corollary worth recording: with this term, λ → ∞
reproduces the prior **exactly** — the design note's own §5 claim, which was
not literally true under the pure D² penalty (whose λ → ∞ limit is the LS
straight line).

### The one constant, derived

ℓ = 12 keys (`REVERSION_LENGTH_KEYS`). The residual field is generated by
deviations of B(m) around the B_ξ model, whose own characteristic lengths
are the asymptote e-foldings: 1/s_T ≈ 10.8 keys (universal treble) and
1/|s_B| ≈ 12.3 keys (this piano's fit; 15.2 for the medium default). A
trend cannot justifiably be carried past the generating model's correlation
length; 12 keys sits inside that bracket and is one octave — the natural
musical unit. Scale-invariant in key-space (passes the Topological Scrutiny
Test: it shapes the reversion geometry; no amplitude/hardware dependence).

### Implementation

* Engines (b)/(c): the pseudo-observations are expressed through the
  *unchanged* textbook Whittaker interface — residual target 0 and weight
  w₀(λ) at unmeasured keys. The λ grid loop lives in the engine layer;
  `smoothing::whittaker_cv_masked` scores the LOO-CV over **measured keys
  only** (cross-validating a pseudo-point would reward predicting the prior
  back to itself).
* Engine (d): pseudo-rows `x_k = 0` at weight w₀(λ) for keys touched by no
  data row; excluded from the GCV's RSS and effective DOF for the same
  reason.

### Alternatives rejected

* **Truncating the smoothing domain** at the last measured key: introduces a
  step discontinuity in d(m) at the boundary.
* **First-order penalty** (constant extrapolation): contradicts the design
  note's §5 smoothness definition (a first-difference penalty fights the
  Railsback slope itself).
* **Global ridge** (w₀ at *all* keys): also shrinks the fit inside the data
  region, changing in-data behavior the design note assigns to λ alone.

## Decision 2 — Minimum-norm chain gauge (fixes defect 2)

The per-chain offset is unidentifiable from Eq.-6 differences, so its
estimate must come from the prior alone. The minimum-norm (Moore–Penrose)
treatment of an unidentifiable component: per chain, choose the offset that
zeroes the mean of (raw − prior) over the chain's **measured** keys — the
orthogonal projection that removes the gauge freedom without asserting any
per-key fact. No key is pinned; the twelve fabricated residual-0
observations are gone. A4 = 0 remains the single *physical* anchor
(reference pitch), applied globally in `finish()` — a pure vertical shift,
which is the correct home for it. Zero new constants.

## Decision 3 — `CurveInput::default()` invariant

Manual `Default` impl returning 88 `None` slots; doc comment states the
invariant. Trivial.

## Consequences (measured, 87 regenerated captures — diagnostics, not selection evidence; n = 1)

Curve values in cents vs ET (pre-fix → post-fix; engine (a) unchanged, its
A7 = +29.3, C8 = +37.4 is the prior the tail should ride):

| engine | A0 | A7 | C8 | treble ¢/oct | roughness med/max | neg-str flags |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| (b) pre | −69.3 | +16.9 | +23.9 | 9.79 | 0.081 / 0.903 | 0 |
| (b) post | −73.8 | **+28.9** | **+37.2** | 15.26 | 0.129 / 0.534 | 6 (see below) |
| (c) pre | −37.3 | +21.9 | +29.7 | 12.40 | 0.065 / 0.511 | 0 |
| (c) post | −39.7 | +32.6 | +41.5 | 17.32 | 0.084 / 0.296 | 0 |
| (d) pre | −18.3 | +21.2 | +28.6 | 11.42 | 0.054 / 0.480 | 0 |
| (d) post | −18.3 | +29.5 | +37.8 | 15.17 | 0.054 / 0.480 | 0 |

* **The tail artifact is gone**: (b)'s A7/C8 now sit at the prior (28.9 vs
  29.3; 37.2 vs 37.4). (c)/(d) likewise ride their asymptotes.
* **DOF-growth improved**: the k-subset sweep (4/8/16/32/87 keys) now reads
  |d−full| = 2.04/1.40/1.34/1.34/0.00 ¢ (pre-fix 5.51/3.03/3.48/1.97/0.00) —
  sparse-profile curves are markedly more stable, exactly the reversion's
  purpose.
* **Six negative-stretch flags appeared on (b)** — investigated and kept.
  They are three octave pairs at the data boundary (44→56, 45→57, 46→58)
  with violations of only −0.14…−0.34 ¢. Cause: the gauge fix un-pinned the
  mid residual, exposing a real, data-driven ~2 ¢ descent across keys 50–56
  (previously masked by the fabricated anchor zeros); where that trend turns
  around at the boundary, three tight mid pairs (prior stretch +3.5 ¢) dip
  fractionally negative. The detector is doing its §2 job — flag, never
  clamp — and the standing remedy (more captures above key 55) is exactly
  what the flag semantics recommend. Suppressing the flags by tuning λ or
  masking would be the banned anti-pattern.
* **Leave-one-key-out numbers are not comparable pre/post**: the LKO
  reference is the raw octave chain, whose gauge changed with Decision 2.
  The yardstick moved with the fix; cross-set comparisons must use the
  post-fix baseline (bass/mid: a 3.41/2.26, b 2.74/1.67, c 12.68/1.63,
  d 23.60/1.84).
* Engine (a) and the (d) data region are byte-stable (cross-check: (a)'s
  Giordano cross-score unchanged at 4.2846).

## Post-acceptance characterization of the six flags (same day, bounded)

A follow-up diagnostic (temporary probe, deleted after this record; threshold
variants built by *profile surgery* — padding/truncating partial counts so
`calculated_b`, and hence the B_ξ fit, is identical across variants) closed
the open questions on the six flags:

1. **The mid-residual descent is data-real and localized.** Measured B runs
   coherently ~20 % *below* the per-instrument fit across keys 40–51
   (ln(B/B_ξ) = −0.13…−0.24, nine consecutive keys), then flips *above* it
   (+0.05…+0.19) from key 52 upward. Because the lower-of-pair B dominates
   the Eq.-6 stretch (weight 4ρ²−1 vs ρ²−1), the below-fit zone drags the
   chained stretches down through exactly the observed descent. This is a
   localized misfit of the two-asymptote B_ξ model in its bridge-crossover
   zone (where the two exponentials cross and the 2-parameter bass pair has
   the least freedom) — or a MAT estimation-bias transition there; n = 1
   cannot distinguish real string design from estimator bias (the standing
   instrument-#2 gate, now localized and quantified).
2. **The flags chase the trust boundary.** Emulated `CURVE_B_MIN_PARTIALS`
   sweep: threshold 5 → boundary key 81, **0 flags**; threshold 8 (shipped)
   → boundary 56, 3 flags; threshold 12 → boundary 47, **0 flags**. The
   shipped threshold parks the boundary at the B-deviation's sign flip
   (keys 51–52), maximizing the descend-then-turn-around geometry. The
   threshold-5 run also shows the flags resolving *by data* when more keys
   are trusted — precisely the §2 remedy. Flags stay; semantics correct.
3. **Not a λ artifact.** The LOO-CV minimum is λ = 31.6 (where the flags
   live; min pair −0.340 ¢); flags persist for every λ ≤ 31.6 and vanish at
   λ = 100, which scores only ~1.5 % worse on a shallow CV curve.
   Suppressing them via λ would be the banned tune-to-kill-a-flag move.
4. **New quantified finding:** the treble target level is
   threshold-sensitive at the ±5 ¢ scale — A7 = +33.5 / +28.9 / +23.3 ¢ at
   thresholds 5/8/12 (the above-fit measured B of keys 52+ raises the
   curve when trusted; the prior alone holds it lower). This (a) upgrades
   the repeat-capture B-variance research item from "nice to have" to the
   blocker for a principled threshold, and (b) gives empirical motivation
   to the design note §8's already-noted refinement candidate — blending
   measured B with the fit weighted by pair count instead of
   hard-switching (a soft handover has no boundary to land badly). Both
   remain gated exactly as recorded there; nothing changed in code.
